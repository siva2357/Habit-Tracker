# Project Synopsis
**TrackIt – Personal Life Management System**

TrackIt is a modern cross-platform desktop application that enables users to organise, track, and manage every important aspect of their personal and professional life from a single, unified platform.

Rather than focusing only on habit tracking, TrackIt combines multiple productivity modules—including daily habits, day planning, personal goals, professional projects, fitness management, and personal finance—into one intuitive workspace.

The application provides users with a clear overview of their daily activities, long-term goals, and overall progress through interactive dashboards, visual analytics, reminders, and structured planning tools. By centralising life management into one application, TrackIt helps users stay organised, improve consistency, increase productivity, and make informed decisions based on their personal data.

Designed with a modern desktop experience in mind, TrackIt offers a clean, responsive interface with support for both light and dark themes, allowing users to focus on what matters most while maintaining complete control over their daily routines and long-term objectives.

## Core Modules

### Dashboard
A complete overview of the user's activities, progress, reminders, and performance across all modules.

### Habit Tracker
Track daily habits, maintain streaks, monitor consistency, and analyse monthly and yearly progress.

### Day Planner
Plan and organise daily schedules using a structured timetable with tasks, priorities, and reminders.

### Goal Planner
Set, organise, and monitor personal, career, education, finance, and lifestyle goals with milestone tracking.

### My Projects
Manage professional and personal projects, tasks, milestones, deadlines, and overall progress.

### My Fitness
Track workouts, body measurements, weight, hydration, sleep, nutrition, and overall health progress.

### My Finances
Manage income, expenses, budgets, savings, recurring payments, and financial reports.

## Application Theme

**Design Style:**
- Modern desktop application
- Minimalistic and clean
- Inspired by Windows 11 and macOS
- Smooth animations
- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Glassmorphism (subtle)
- Spacious layout
- Responsive design
- Light and Dark themes

**Navigation Style:**
After logging in, users arrive at a Home Screen featuring large interactive cards for each module. This serves as the central hub of the application, making navigation simple and visually engaging. Once a module is selected, a consistent layout with a top header and optional left navigation provides access to module-specific features.

This structure positions TrackIt as more than a habit tracker—it becomes a complete Personal Life Management System designed to help users plan, track, and improve every major area of their lives.
